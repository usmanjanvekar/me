### Usman Janvekar · Founder & CEO, [Otacta](https://otacta.ai) · Toronto

Otacta builds self-calibrating decision systems.

Our product, **Sofia**, makes the recurring operating decisions that manufacturers re-do every week (pricing, inventory, demand, production), then measures what each decision actually caused and recalibrates the method behind the next one. The layer underneath, the **Live Decision Model**, is what persists and compounds from cycle to cycle.

Before Otacta, I spent a decade as analytics lead at Haleon, Allergan and Sanofi, building the pricing, demand and marketing-mix models behind commercial decisions, across consumer health manufacturing and retail, and re-fitting them by hand every cycle.
